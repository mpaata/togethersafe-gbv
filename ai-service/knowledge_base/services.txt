**EMERGENCY & POLICE**
Organization: Uganda Police Force (Emergency)
Category: Police
Location: National
Phone: 999
Description: 24/7 emergency response for any GBV incident. Ask to be connected to the Child and Family Protection Unit (CFPU) if the case involves a child or domestic violence.

Organization: Child and Family Protection Unit (CFPU) - Uganda Police
Category: Police
Location: National (operates in districts like Tororo, Lira, Hoima, Kiryandongo)
Phone: 999 (Ask for CFPU)
Description: Specialized unit within the Uganda Police that handles sexual and gender-based violence, domestic violence, and child protection cases [citation:13].
